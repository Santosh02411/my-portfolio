About

Belagavi, KA

I'm a final-year Computer Science Engineering graduate from Maratha Mandal's Engineering College, Belagavi — CGPA 8.67/10 — and a software engineer who likes owning a system end to end: backend, API, database, frontend, and whatever ML sits inside it.

Most of what I build is full-stack: REST APIs with OAuth and multi-service integrations (a six-platform social ads dashboard), offline-first sync architecture with local persistence and conflict resolution (Delivery Sync), and React/TypeScript frontends backed by Node.js or Flask. I care about the parts of engineering that don't show up in a demo — auth, data modeling, deployment — as much as the feature itself.

Alongside that, I build and ship ML systems: end-to-end pipelines from ingestion through deployment during my internship at EchoBrains Technologies, and agentic LLM applications with LangChain and RAG as a top-ranked fellow in VTU's Bharat Unnati AI program. I like ML best when it's wired into something a user actually touches, not left in a notebook. When I'm not coding, I'm usually gaming, watching a K-drama or series, traveling, or sketching out the next project idea.
